# Player pose and identity

The current local profile uses **RTMPose-m**, a SimCC pose model from OpenMMLab, through an original OpenCV DNN adapter. It estimates the 17 COCO body joints. Preprocessing uses a 1.25 padded, aspect-preserving 192×256 crop, BGR mean/std normalization and the released graph's 2× SimCC coordinates. Crop offsets and scale are inverted before rendering. A mean axis response below 0.30 leaves a joint missing. These scores are model responses, not calibrated probabilities. No inferred 3D joint coordinates are presented as measured anatomy.

SSDLite proposes people in overlapping image crops, with extra high-resolution searches around the calibrated far baseline. Artificially truncated tile detections are discarded. Near/far assignment uses court position, temporal proximity and an initial center-court prior; it does not recognize a person's identity. The playing-region filter rejects distant spectators and sideline coaches. Between detector refreshes, forward/backward Lucas–Kanade checks measure box motion; failure triggers fresh detection. RTMPose runs on the current player crop, including these intervening frames. The source of a box is recorded.

Where both ankles are supported, their approximate footpoint replaces the bottom of the person box. Raw box footpoints remain in `raw_xy_px`. Feet can be airborne, so player ground speed/path remain approximate. Missing joints are never drawn as an invented skeleton. Knee angles are **2D image angles**, affected by perspective and occlusion; they are not 3D biomechanics.

Skeleton overlays appear in uploaded-video playback, sampled live-camera analysis and annotated MP4. `keypoints.csv` and version 1.3 JSON retain each available joint and score. Live camera person detection and checked flow support current-frame pose within each sampled burst; this does not imply continuous full-rate capture. Raw joint coordinates remain alongside One Euro filtered display coordinates. Filters reset on missing joints or gaps over 150 ms.

Primary references: [RTMPose paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07399), [OpenMMLab project](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmpose/tree/main/projects/rtmpose), [maintainer's RTMLib preprocessing](https://github.com/Tau-J/rtmlib/blob/main/rtmlib/tools/pose_estimation/rtmpose.py). The downloaded graph, revision and hashes are in `MODEL_MANIFEST.yaml`. Upstream project code is Apache-2.0; pretrained data terms remain separate. We do not redistribute the checkpoint or imply a blanket license over its training datasets.

The previously evaluated MediaPipe crop baseline is superseded. The [CourtSide v1 model card](https://huggingface.co/Davidsv/CourtSide-Computer-Vision-v1) lists ball, racket and court-zone detection classes, without person body-keypoint outputs. Its card alone cannot supply the pose feature in the user's screenshot.
